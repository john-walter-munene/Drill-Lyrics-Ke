# Code of Conduct

DrillKe exists for a music scene built by young people, often in neighbourhoods
the rest of the internet ignores. Behave like a guest in their house.

## Expected

- Be respectful and constructive. Critique ideas, not people.
- Explain rather than dismiss. Many contributors are here because they love the
  music, not because they write code.
- Respect artist rights and privacy. Do not post someone's address, phone
  number or identifying details.

## Not tolerated

- Harassment, threats, hate speech or slurs.
- Doxxing, or sharing private information about contributors, artists or fans.
- Glorifying or organising real-world violence.
- Deliberate copyright infringement, or uploading content you have no right to.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove content, close contributions, or ban participants. Serious
issues are documented and, where appropriate, reported.

Report concerns privately to the maintainers. Reports are handled confidentially.
